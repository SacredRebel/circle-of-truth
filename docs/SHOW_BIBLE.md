# CIRCLE OF TRUTH — SHOW BIBLE

## Series identity

**Working format:** 20–30-minute premium animated fictional roundtable / conversation series, built with reusable cast, sets, scripts and cameras. Theme: *a conversation beyond the noise*. Cinematic but intimate, warm, thoughtful, lightly humorous and emotionally grounded.

## Canonical pilot premise

An extravagant **fictional** corporate/political gala or boardroom is interrupted by a quiet visitor: Jesus, wearing simple humble linen. Confident conversation turns into stunned silence. He gently says hello, invites selected guests to follow him, and walks them into a timeless circular conversation space with **a low central fire and equal-height chairs**—no throne, pulpit or literal classroom. He opens, 'How is everyone doing?' The conversation unfolds organically about power, fear, love, compassion, responsibility, healing and truth.

### Character principles

Jesus listens, responds and jokes; he is not omniscient narration on every line. Guests may feel awkward, curious, funny and vulnerable rather than being caricatured or humiliated. Respect spiritual and historical traditions; do not present invented religious dialogue as authentic revelations.

### Visual language

- Mature, original, stylized 3D; original character design, consistent anatomy and expressive eyes.
- Elite hall: dark oak, brass, stone, large original art, cooler dramatic lighting.
- Roundtable: textured stone/wood, equal-height movable chairs, intimate orange firelight.
- Cast-sheet artwork: **exactly 10 people, each once, one row, full length including feet and shoes**, with full names and public roles below, centered gold Tree of Life, delicate sacred geometry, blue/gold night and waterfalls.
- Four reusable shots: wide circular master, medium single, listening reaction, occasional dramatic tension close-up; avoid restless camera movement.

### Sound and fiction disclosure

Never imitate or clone real living people's voices. Use clearly fictional performances and never imply an authentic private meeting or allegation. Show near opening and closing: **'An animated work of fiction. Dialogue and portrayals are imagined; no real-world statements or endorsements are implied.'** Apply YouTube's altered/synthetic-content disclosure as relevant.

## Production gate order

Three-character 12–20s test → 45–60s intro → 3-minute prototype → approved 20–30-minute pilot. No full-length video, rig or published episodes have been demonstrated in this repository yet. Consult `docs/HERMES_MASTER_PRODUCTION_BRIEF.md` for detailed technical implementation notes.
