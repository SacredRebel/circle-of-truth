## The founding concept — what the PDF actually proposes

- **Format / scale (p.3):** 15 episodes, '90 MINUTES' in the format bar, 12 principal guests, two truth topics, 1–2 live performances, 1–2 regenerative practices, and a spotlight on a collaborative-impact project. The precise runtime meaning of '90 MINUTES' is unverified.
- **Host (p.3, p.10):** Mark Pancis is named as the show host/director. The pitch positions the format as three ceremonial days culminating in a real **Circle of Truth**.
- **Editorial DNA (p.3):** Healing arts, ancient and contemporary knowledge, natural systems and actionable regenerative practice across personal and community scales.
- **Eight ACTS, not eight episodes (pp.5–6):** Ancient Future / Native Roots; All of Our Relations; Regeneration & Collaboration; Sacred Rebellion; Quantum Reality; Divine Masculine and Feminine; Our Bodies, Our Sacred Temple; AI and the Ethics of Illusion.
- **Casting wishlist (pp.7–8):** 24 named *potential* guests, not confirmed bookings. Source spellings include 'Joe Rogen', 'Jason Mamoa', 'Ian Somerholder' and 'vandanashiva'; the structured data records both raw and normalized names.
- **Location (p.9):** Awhai / Ojai, California, positioned as a spiritually resonant regenerative production setting. Cultural, historical, Indigenous and land claims in that page must be fact-checked and consent-reviewed before being presented as fact.
- **Team (p.10):** Pitch-era names and roles, including Mark Pancis, Kim Dudine, Jonathan Braniff and D. Todd Shepherd, and a *potential* Jerry Bruckheimer lead producer slot. This is not an assertion of current commitments.
- **Distribution (p.12):** YouTube, Rumble, linear/OTT and social featurettes.
- **Imagery (pp.1,4,13,14):** Ember/flame silhouettes, spaces of silence, hand-to-hand ceremonies, natural textured architecture, ritual circles, land regeneration and intimate human presence.
- **Schedule (p.15):** Full-moon filming 2026–2027, with a historical January–Spring 2026 startup/kickoff plan. This is outdated as a forward-looking production promise; it is retained as historical context only.
- **Pilot investment & related visions (p.16):** Historical proposal of $1.1M for three pilot shows, further original-film concepts and regenerative network/media infrastructure. Not verified financing.
- **Contribution tiers (pp.17–19):** Historical concept sponsorship levels at $50k, $100k, $250k, $500k and $1M+, with draft brand placements, memberships and impact promises. Do not put these on an active checkout or investment page without contracts, disclosures and legal review.
- **Personal contact page (p.20):** Source contains individual contact info; deliberately not exposed in this public web adaptation.

## Comparison to current repository

The existing site and files presently center a distinct **fictional 3D animated format**: elite gala → Jesus invites Obama/Trump to a circle → shared fire and equal chairs → human dialogue. Its proposed episodes are 20–30 minutes, and its first true deliverable is a 12–20 second proof. The new PDF predates and broadens this concept. It is **not** a script for the animated series. Merging every field into one show would misrepresent both.

## Recommended canonical architecture

1. **Circle of Truth — THE ORIGINAL SERIES** (live, ceremonial, documentary / unscripted; founding flagship). Preserve 15-episode ambition, 8-act editorial arc, tentative Circle of 12, Ojai and ritual/impact elements as *proposed*.
2. **Circle of Truth — THE ANIMATED UNIVERSE** (fictional thought-experiment companion). Preserve premium cinematic 3D original characters, equal-seat fire circle, 102-record casting library, short-to-long Blender/Remotion path.
3. **Impact / Field Notes** (future layer). Only documentary-cleared projects, practical steps, verified land and regeneration research, partner stories with permission; not an automatically committed venture or ownership claim.
4. **Production Studio** (internal area). Catalog, assets, storyboard, live/animation pipelines, roles, distribution plans, issues and version control.

## Critical safeguards

- A public guest wishlist does not establish invitation, appearance, endorsement or permission.
- Claiming strong outcomes for healing modalities requires evidence and appropriate medical qualifications.
- Ceremony, ancestral/Indigenous knowledge and community representation require permission and contextual integrity.
- The deck includes claims about Ojai spiritual history, audience reach, tax deductibility and capital investment that are unverified.
- Raw mood-board imagery may be third-party copyrighted; use original custom graphics and art whose rights have been verified.
- Sponsor tiers are *archived pitch figures*, not purchasable live products or financial offers.
