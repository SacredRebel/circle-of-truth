# Circle of Truth — Artwork Asset Register

## Status codes

- **approved**: explicitly praised/approved by the creator, not necessarily final publication/legal clearance.
- **rendered**: generated and associated with a correct planned roster; pending detailed QA.
- **review**: existing research artwork without cast-by-cast confirmation.
- **rejected**: incorrect people, title, or layout. NEVER use as an approved collection image.
- **missing**: an approved roster with no correct poster yet.
