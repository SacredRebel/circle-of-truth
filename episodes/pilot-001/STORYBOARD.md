# Pilot 001 — The Invitation

**Status:** Technical storyboard, not a finished screenplay. **P0 cast:** Jesus, Barack Obama, Donald Trump. **Format goal:** 20–30 minutes, but approve only a 12–20 second technical test first.

## Story spine

**World 1 — The gala.** Extravagant paintings, luxurious furniture, a privileged gathering and confident cross-talk. Jesus quietly enters in rough, simple linen. People notice, become silent, glance toward one another.

**Invitation.** Jesus smiles and says, 'Good evening.' A beat. 'Don't let me interrupt. You seemed very busy.' After the room has settled: 'Come with me.' No accusation, sermon or forced confession.

**World 2 — The circle.** A low fire, simple chairs at equal height. No throne. He looks at them with warmth: 'Well. How is everyone doing?' A long enough quiet to become funny: 'You were a lot more talkative a minute ago.' Everyone begins speaking like actual nervous humans.
